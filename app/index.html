The person behind the chair

June Wilde

Hi, I'm June. I started Wren & Wilde in my garage in 2013.

Eleven years ago, I was cutting hair out of a converted garage because it was hard to find a salon that didn't rush the consultation. Wren & Wilde grew out of that frustration: a place where the first fifteen minutes are just conversation, no scissors in sight.

Today it's me and three stylists I trained myself, in a studio full of plants I probably over-water. We keep a sustainable client list, and work with it strategically so we actually know your hair.
